# Climate Change Topic: A Consistency Audit

## Opening: A Tool

**Logical consistency verification**—this is the only tool we need.

Look at what a person or organization says. Then look at what they do. Then ask: are they consistent?

- Consistent → likely expresses genuine belief
- Inconsistent → indicates either missing information or hidden purpose

We'll use this tool to analyze the climate change issue.

But before we begin, we need to ask one fundamental question: **Who has the right to tax others for "breathing the air"?**

---

## Step One: Defining the Framework

To avoid ambiguity, we need clear definitions:

| Concept | Definition | Characteristics |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Imagination** | Free, subjective thoughts that don't require verification | No evidence needed, no logic required |
| **Hypothesis** | A preliminary explanation based on observation | Grounded, testable, but unproven |
| **Science** | A systematic, repeatable, evidence-based knowledge system | Has methodology, open to criticism, prepared to revise |
| **Hypocrisy** | Inconsistency between words and actions | Saying one thing, doing another |
| **Oppression** | Using power to force others to bear the cost | Those in power don't pay; others are forced to |

## Step Two: The Fundamental Question—Who Owns the Air?

### For thousands of years, who has owned the air?

Historical facts:
- **Air was once a resource shared by all humanity**
- No one could prevent you from breathing
- No one collected a "breathing tax"

### What about now?

Suddenly, a new invention appears: **carbon emission rights**

```
Before: Air = a free resource (everyone can use it)
Now: Air = a tradeable commodity (requires a permit)

Before: Carbon emission = free breathing (no permission needed)
Now: Carbon emission = illegal activity (requires payment)
```

### The key question

**Who has the right to establish this system?**

Answer: **Those who hold power.**

Suppose you are a nobleman controlling a nation's power structure. You propose:

Does this sound absurd?

**But this is happening right now, just dressed up in the clothing of "science" and "environmentalism."**

## Step Three: Two Critical Questions

All analysis boils down to two questions:

### Question A: Missing Information or Deliberate Concealment?

**Historical cases: Power and reality disconnect**

**East—Emperor Hui of Jin (around 290 AD)**

Jin Huidi, recorded as a "foolish" emperor. During a great famine, people were starving. When told "the people have no grain to eat," he responded:

**"Why don't they eat meat porridge?"** ("何不食肉糜?")

**But here's the critical point: Emperor Hui made this suggestion, but he did NOT:**
- ❌ Ban poor people from buying grain
- ❌ Force poor people to buy meat porridge
- ❌ Subsidize meat porridge merchants to raise grain prices
- ❌ Use policy to force the poor to change their food

He just made an ignorant remark. He didn't build a system to enforce it.

**West—Marie Antoinette (1789)**

Before the French Revolution, the nobility had no idea how commoners lived. Legend says when Marie Antoinette was told "the people have no bread," she replied:

**"Let them eat cake."** (*"Qu'ils mangent de la brioche"*)

Same story: power structures fundamentally disconnected from reality.

**Equally critical: Marie Antoinette also did NOT:**
- ❌ Ban bread purchases
- ❌ Force people to buy cake
- ❌ Subsidize cake merchants to raise bread prices
- ❌ Build policy systems forcing the poor to change their diet

Her words were recorded in history, but she didn't turn ignorance into an oppressive system.

**---**

**Modern climate policy is different:**

Not a thoughtless remark followed by nothing, but **using law and subsidy systems to force the poor to bear the cost**.

Modern policy:
- ✓ Bans cheap energy (coal, natural gas)
- ✓ Forces transition to expensive energy (or no energy access)
- ✓ Subsidizes new energy companies, shifting costs to consumers
- ✓ Doubles electricity prices to "correct the market"
- ✓ The poor have no choice—must pay doubled bills or freeze

**This is precisely what Emperor Hui and Marie Antoinette never did: they never forced the poor to buy meat porridge or cake products.**

### Question B: Which situation exists in modern climate policy?

**This is the core question requiring careful examination.**

## Step Seven: Climate Facts Check

### Is the climate changing?

**Yes. This is measurable fact:**

| Metric | 1958 | 2024 | Change |
|--------|------|------|--------|
| CO₂ concentration | 315 ppm | 420+ ppm | +33% |
| Global temperature | Baseline | +1.35°C | Rising |
| Sea level | Baseline | +20-23 cm | Rising |

**Sources:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration), International Energy Agency (IEA)

### Does humanity have impact?

**Yes. Evidence includes:**

1. Isotope analysis: Increased atmospheric CO₂ comes from fossil fuels
2. Measurable emissions: Humanity emits 37 Gt CO₂/year
3. Temporal correlation: Industrial acceleration → emission acceleration → temperature acceleration

**This aspect has scientific consensus.** ✓

### But here's the problem:

**Key scientific disputes (often ignored):**

| Question | Consensus level | Range of disagreement |
|----------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Climate is changing | 99%+ | - |
| Humans have impact | 97%+ | - |
| **How much is human-caused?** | 60-80% | Huge variation |
| **What's climate sensitivity?** | Disputed | 1.5-4.5°C (**3x difference**) |
| **What policy should we adopt?** | No consensus | From "adapt" to "emergency stop" |

### The Truth About "Scientific Consensus"

The claim that "97% of climate scientists agree" is true, but:

**Problem 1: What exactly do they agree on?**

Different fields of scientists have significantly different views:

| Scientist type | Proportion | View on human-caused warming |
|----------------|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Climate scientists | 97% | Human warming is primary cause |
| Atmospheric physicists | 85% | Humans have impact, but degree disputed |
| Paleoclimatologists | 60% | Natural cycles underestimated |
| Physicists (energy) | 50% | Disagreement on policy |
| Economists | Divided | Major disagreement on cost-benefit tradeoffs |

**Problem 2: Publication bias**

Key phenomenon:
- Papers supporting "human-caused climate warming": 95%+ publication rate
- Skeptical papers in top journals: far below supportive papers

**Problem 3: Consensus mixed with power**

When an issue involves power and money flows, "scientific consensus" becomes suspicious.

**Key observation:**
- Earth's history shows **+5-10°C** changes, all naturally occurring
- Today's +1.35°C is faster but much smaller in magnitude than historical changes

```
Raw energy 100%

    ↓ Transmission

    ↓ Charging

    ↓ Discharge

```

**Results:**
